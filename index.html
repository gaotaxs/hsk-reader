Install & offline

On iPhone or iPad, open this site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On desktop, use your browser’s install option.

Checking offline availability…

Updates wait until you choose to reload. Repair refreshes this app’s cached files; it keeps study progress. Online translation still needs a connection. Voice availability depends on your device.

Speech and translation

Online translation sends pasted text to MyMemory. Turn it off to use the built-in word glossary only.

Stroke order

On iPad, select Trace practice in a word popup or flashcard, then write inside the enlarged pad with an Apple Pencil or finger. All 622 dictionary characters are bundled for offline use once the app is downloaded. Palm rejection depends on iPadOS and the browser; physical Apple Pencil testing is still required.

Progress backup

Known/learning status, lesson metadata, and settings are saved in this browser's localStorage.

Your progress

Progress is kept locally and can be lost when changing browsers, preview apps or file locations. Download a backup regularly; it now includes the current reader text.

Keyboard shortcuts and tips

Global: Alt+1–6 change tabs, / focuses vocabulary search, Esc closes word popup.
